### Descriptions

- **O1-KR1.t1**: Identify and document potential lead generation channels suitable for reaching Vietnamese SMEs in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. This includes online platforms (LinkedIn, Facebook, industry forums), offline events, partnerships, and content marketing opportunities. Prioritize channels based on potential reach and cost-effectiveness.
- **O1-KR1.t2**: Review and refine the existing ICP to ensure it accurately reflects the characteristics of Vietnamese SMEs most likely to benefit from the HR software. Consider industry, size, location, and pain points. Document any changes to the ICP.
- **O1-KR1.t3**: Create a valuable lead magnet (e.g., ebook, checklist, webinar) tailored to the needs and pain points of the refined ICP. Ensure the content is relevant, engaging, and provides actionable insights. Translate into Vietnamese if necessary.
- **O1-KR1.t4**: Execute a lead generation campaign on at least one of the prioritized channels identified in task t1, utilizing the lead magnet created in task t3. This includes setting up tracking, creating ad copy, and managing campaign budget. Focus on a single channel initially for testing and optimization.
- **O1-KR1.t5**: Continuously monitor the performance of the lead generation campaign, tracking key metrics such as impressions, clicks, conversion rates, and cost per lead. Make adjustments to ad copy, targeting, and bidding strategies to improve performance. Document changes and results.
- **O1-KR1.t6**: Based on the results of the initial campaign, evaluate the effectiveness of the chosen channel and identify opportunities to expand to other channels. Prioritize channels based on potential ROI and alignment with the ICP.
- **O1-KR2.t1**: Research and segment Vietnamese SMEs in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City based on industry, size, and HR software needs. Prioritize segments with the highest potential for annual contract sign-ups. Document findings and rationale.
- **O1-KR2.t2**: Based on the segment prioritization, refine the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) specifically for SMEs likely to commit to annual contracts.  Detail key characteristics, pain points, and decision-making processes.
- **O1-KR2.t3**: Craft compelling messaging tailored to the refined ICP, highlighting the value proposition of annual contracts (e.g., cost savings, dedicated support, strategic partnership). Create multiple message variations for different channels.
- **O1-KR2.t4**: Collaborate with the sales team to review current strategies and adapt them to focus on securing annual contracts. Provide training and support on the new messaging and ICP.  Document adjustments.
- **O1-KR2.t5**: Implement the targeted outreach campaign to prioritized segments. Diligently track engagement metrics (opens, clicks, replies, meetings booked) and identify areas for optimization.  Document findings.
- **O1-KR2.t6**: Analyze the data from the initial outreach phase. Refine messaging, targeting, and channel selection based on performance. Document changes and rationale.
- **O1-KR2.t7**: Continue outreach efforts using the refined approach. Focus on nurturing leads and guiding them through the contract negotiation process. Track progress towards the 8 annual contract target.
- **O1-KR2.t8**: Focus on closing remaining deals and securing the final annual contracts needed to reach the target. Address any roadblocks and provide necessary support to the sales team.
- **O1-KR3.t1**: Review the last 6-12 months of closed deals (both successful and unsuccessful) with Vietnamese SMEs. Identify common characteristics of deals that exceeded $2,000 first-year value versus those below. Segment customer profiles to understand what factors correlate with higher deal sizes. Document findings and initial hypotheses.
- **O1-KR3.t2**: Based on the deal analysis, refine the ICP to specifically target Vietnamese SMEs with a higher propensity for larger first-year deals. This includes firmographic, technographic, and behavioral criteria. Document the revised ICP.
- **O1-KR3.t3**: Based on the refined ICP, develop adjustments to the value proposition to better resonate with these higher-value segments. Focus on highlighting features and benefits that justify a larger investment. Document these adjustments.
- **O1-KR3.t4**: Update the sales playbook with the revised ICP and value proposition adjustments. Conduct a brief training session with the sales team (3 people) to ensure they understand and can apply the new approach. Document the training session.
- **O1-KR3.t5**: For the next month, closely monitor the average first-year deal size. Track deals closed using the updated playbook.  Identify any unexpected results or areas for further refinement. Document observations and potential adjustments to the strategy.
- **O2-KR1.t1**: Outline the key sections of the sales playbook (e.g., prospecting, qualification, demo, negotiation, closing, onboarding). Define the level of detail required in each section. Consider existing sales documentation and team input.
- **O2-KR1.t2**: Collect all existing sales documentation (email templates, call scripts, presentations, case studies, proposal templates). Interview sales team members to capture their best practices and successful approaches.
- **O2-KR1.t3**: Based on the structure and gathered materials, draft the initial content for the prospecting and qualification sections of the playbook. Focus on clear, actionable steps and examples.
- **O2-KR1.t4**: Draft the initial content for the demo, negotiation, and closing sections of the playbook. Focus on clear, actionable steps and examples.
- **O2-KR1.t5**: Share the draft playbook with the sales team and solicit feedback. Schedule a meeting to discuss and gather input.
- **O2-KR1.t6**: Incorporate the feedback received from the sales team into the playbook. Refine the content and ensure clarity and accuracy.
- **O2-KR1.t7**: Perform a final review of the playbook. Determine the best method for distributing and accessing the playbook (e.g., shared drive, internal wiki).
- **O2-KR1.t8**: Conduct a training session for the sales team on how to use the sales playbook. Ensure everyone understands the content and can apply it to their sales process.
- **O2-KR2.t1**: Examine the current CRM data to pinpoint which fields are frequently missing. Categorize missing fields by importance and potential impact on sales effectiveness. Document findings.
- **O2-KR2.t2**: Based on the analysis, design rules and validations within the CRM to enforce completion of critical fields. Consider user experience and avoid overly restrictive rules that hinder adoption. Document the proposed rules.
- **O2-KR2.t3**: Implement the designed data validation rules within the CRM system. Test the rules thoroughly to ensure they function as expected and don't negatively impact data entry.
- **O2-KR2.t4**: Conduct a brief training session for the sales team explaining the new CRM data validation rules and their importance. Address any questions or concerns.
