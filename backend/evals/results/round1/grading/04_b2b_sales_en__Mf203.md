### Descriptions

- **O1-KR1.t1**: Research and reach out to 15 SMEs in the target regions that fit the ideal customer profile. Use LinkedIn, industry directories, and referrals to identify prospects. Qualify them based on company size, industry, and pain points.
- **O1-KR1.t2**: Follow up with the 15 qualified leads from the previous task to schedule discovery calls. Use the calls to understand their business challenges, evaluate their fit, and build rapport.
- **O1-KR1.t3**: Based on the discovery call insights, create 15 tailored proposals that address the specific needs of each lead. Ensure the proposals are clear, concise, and include a clear call to action.
- **O1-KR1.t4**: Monitor the response to the proposals and follow up with each lead to address any questions, clarify the value proposition, and move them further down the sales funnel.
- **O1-KR2.t1**: Research and compile a list of 20 SMEs in the target industries (e.g., manufacturing, retail, logistics) in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City that are likely to benefit from the HR software. Use LinkedIn, industry reports, and local business directories to identify potential leads.
- **O1-KR2.t2**: Craft and send personalized email campaigns to the 20 SMEs identified in t1. Focus on highlighting the value proposition of the HR software, including cost savings, efficiency, and scalability for SMEs.
- **O1-KR2.t3**: Follow up on the emails sent in t2 and schedule 10 initial calls with SMEs. During the calls, understand their pain points, business needs, and how the HR software can help them.
- **O1-KR2.t4**: From the 10 initial calls, identify 5 SMEs that are interested in moving forward. Prepare tailored proposals and negotiate terms, including pricing, contract duration, and onboarding.
- **O1-KR2.t5**: Complete the contract signing process for the 5 SMEs in t4. Ensure all terms are clear, and the contracts are signed and recorded in the CRM.
- **O1-KR3.t1**: Review past deals to understand the current distribution of deal sizes and identify patterns or opportunities to increase average deal size.
- **O1-KR3.t2**: Research and define segments of Vietnamese SMEs that are more likely to commit to larger deals, based on industry, size, or needs.
- **O1-KR3.t3**: Create strategies to upsell or cross-sell additional products or services to existing clients to increase deal size.
- **O1-KR3.t4**: Set up a system to track deal sizes and monitor progress toward the target average deal size.
- **O1-KR3.t5**: Train the sales team on techniques to close larger deals and improve average deal size.
- **O2-KR1.t1**: Outline the core components of the sales playbook, including lead qualification, outreach strategies, objection handling, and closing techniques.
- **O2-KR1.t2**: Write detailed content for each section of the playbook, including templates, scripts, and best practices for the sales team.
- **O2-KR1.t3**: Host a session with the sales team to review the playbook, gather feedback, and make necessary revisions.
- **O2-KR1.t4**: Finalize the playbook, ensure all content is accurate and consistent, and distribute it to the entire sales team.
- **O2-KR2.t1**: Review current CRM data to identify which required fields are missing and calculate the current completeness percentage.
- **O2-KR2.t2**: List all required fields in the CRM and determine which ones are not being filled consistently by the sales team.
- **O2-KR2.t3**: Develop a checklist of required fields that must be filled for each sales lead and assign ownership to the sales team members.
- **O2-KR2.t4**: Conduct a training session to ensure the sales team understands the checklist and the importance of data completeness.
- **O2-KR2.t5**: Set up a system to track CRM data completeness on a weekly basis and provide feedback to the team.
- **O2-KR2.t6**: Review the data completeness metrics weekly and make adjustments to the checklist or training as needed.
